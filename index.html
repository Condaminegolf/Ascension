YOUR FIVE-DAY TRAINING PLAN

Customise this training day

Change exercises, set counts and rep targets. Your five training days remain available. Previously logged workouts are never rewritten.

Saving changes clears unsaved set drafts for this day, after confirmation.

Check each set as you finish it. Enter the weight and repetitions actually performed. Save any number of completed sets when time runs out. You can return and finish the remaining sets later. XP is awarded only for newly saved sets; the session bonus is proportional to completed sets. Rank also requires cumulative reps, kg lifted (weight × reps), and from C Rank upward, bodyweight-relative strength trials. Inactivity lowers current rank after a 7-day grace period (one tier per 14 further days). Regain the first lost tier after 3 distinct active days, then one more tier per 5 additional active days; each tier also requires a workout or cardio day. Attributes lose 5 points per 7 inactive days after the grace period. Lifetime XP and records remain saved.

ASCENSION PATH

From F to S Rank

Every rank requires XP, training sessions, total repetitions and total training volume (kg × reps). C Rank onward also requires strength trials scaled to bodyweight. Partial sessions count and can be continued without double-counting.

Achievement Badges

Strength Trials · Relative to Bodyweight

Bench, squat, overhead press and Romanian deadlift milestones are scaled to your current bodyweight. Your best estimated 1-rep max (e1RM) is calculated from logged sets of 1–10 reps. These are game milestones, not medical or normative strength standards. Never attempt a max lift just to earn a badge.
