Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

ASCENSION is a personal fitness-tracking web application. This notice describes how information is handled by the application as currently deployed, and how planned integrations with Oura and Strava would work if enabled.

Information we collect

When you create an account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and authentication credentials. ASCENSION can store your training profile, workout and exercise sets, cardio activities, bodyweight records, goals, XP, ranks, achievements and app preferences.

Where data is stored

Training data is saved locally in your browser. If you sign in and enable cloud sync, your saved app data is also stored in the ASCENSION Supabase project. Supabase provides authentication and database infrastructure. Data can be transferred between your signed-in devices.

Oura and Strava integrations

Oura and Strava connections are planned and are not yet active in the current version. If enabled in a future release and you explicitly authorise a connection, ASCENSION intends to request only the permissions needed to import Oura sleep/readiness and relevant heart-rate metrics, and Strava run, ride and walk activities. These data would be used for your personal recovery dashboard and cardio history, respectively. Any required OAuth tokens should be stored server-side rather than in the public website. We will update this notice before enabling these integrations if data handling differs from this description.

Use and sharing

Information is used to provide training logs, analytics, progress and syncing. We do not sell your personal or health-related information. Data is processed by service providers necessary to operate the application, including Supabase, and by Oura or Strava only where you separately authorise their services. Their own privacy notices also apply.

Security and limitations

ASCENSION uses authenticated access and database row-level security to restrict cloud saves to their account owners. No online system can guarantee absolute security. Avoid using shared devices without signing out, and maintain JSON backups of important training records.

Retention, access and deletion

You can export your saved training data from the app. Browser data can be cleared from your device; doing so does not automatically delete cloud data. To request access, correction or deletion of cloud account information, contact rumorgan1234@hotmail.com. Requests will be handled manually; the current app does not provide a self-service account-deletion feature. Disconnecting a future third-party integration would stop new imports but may not automatically remove previously imported records.

Contact and changes

Privacy questions: rumorgan1234@hotmail.com. This notice may be updated when integrations or data practices change.
